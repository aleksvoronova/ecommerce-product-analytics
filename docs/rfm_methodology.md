\# RFM Segmentation Methodology



\## Objective



Segment customers according to recency, purchase frequency and historical product value.



The segmentation is designed for descriptive customer analytics and prioritization rather than predictive modeling.



\## Population



Only customers with at least one delivered order are included.



Customer identity is based on `customer\_unique\_id`.



\## Reference Date



The dataset is historical, therefore the current system date is not used.



Reference date is defined as:



`maximum delivered purchase date + 1 day`



This prevents historical customers from being incorrectly classified as inactive simply because the analysis is being executed years after the dataset period.



\## Recency



Recency represents the number of days between the reference date and the customer's most recent delivered purchase.



Lower recency indicates more recent activity.



Recency scores are assigned using distribution-based quintile thresholds.



\- R5 = most recent customers

\- R1 = least recent customers



\## Frequency



Frequency represents the number of distinct delivered orders placed by a customer.



Because the dataset contains a highly discrete and skewed frequency distribution, NTILE is not used for this component. Using NTILE could assign different scores to customers with exactly the same purchase frequency.



Frequency scoring:



\- 1 delivered order → F1

\- 2 delivered orders → F3

\- 3 delivered orders → F4

\- 4+ delivered orders → F5



\## Monetary



Monetary represents total product value generated by a customer:



`SUM(order\_items.price)`



Freight is excluded.



Monetary scores are assigned using distribution-based quintile thresholds.



\- M1 = lowest monetary group

\- M5 = highest monetary group



\## Segments



\### Champions



Recent repeat customers with high monetary value.



\### Loyal Customers



Repeat customers who purchased relatively recently.



\### Potential Loyalists



Recent single-order customers with medium or high monetary value.



\### Recent Customers



Very recent single-order customers with lower historical monetary value.



\### At Risk



Repeat customers whose last purchase occurred relatively long ago.



\### Lost Customers



Old single-order customers with no recent activity.



\### Other



Customers who do not meet the defined rules above.



\## Limitations



The segmentation is descriptive and is based only on historical transactions.



The source data does not contain:



\- marketing acquisition channels;

\- customer acquisition cost;

\- browsing behavior;

\- customer demographics;

\- subscription status;

\- customer-level margin.



Segment thresholds are analytical rules for this project and should not be interpreted as universal business definitions.
